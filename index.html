About PRO STALL (PTY) LTD

BUILDING BETTER SPACES THROUGH QUALITY WORKMANSHIP.

PRO STALL (PTY) LTD provides specialised construction finishing and commercial interior installation solutions, helping businesses and property owners create functional, attractive and professional spaces.

Based in Vosloorus, Gauteng, we specialise in drywalling, suspended ceiling installations, office furniture supply and installation, and modern kitchen units. Every project is driven by quality workmanship, practical solutions, customer satisfaction, and meticulous attention to detail.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to common enquiries regarding our drywalling, ceiling, office furniture, and kitchen installation services.

What services does PRO STALL provide? +

PRO STALL (PTY) LTD specialises in Drywalling Solutions, Suspended Ceiling Installations, Office Furniture Supply & Installation, Kitchen Units & Installations, Commercial Interior Solutions, and Construction & Finishing Solutions.

Do you supply and install drywall partitions? +

Yes. We provide complete supply and professional installation of drywall partitions for offices, commercial buildings, and interior spaces.

Do you install suspended ceilings? +

Yes. We supply and install modern suspended ceiling systems designed to deliver clean, functional, and professional commercial and office interiors.

Do you supply office furniture? +

Yes. We supply and install office furniture tailored to create comfortable, productive, and professional working environments.

Do you install kitchen units? +

Yes. We supply and install modern kitchen units, cabinets, and practical kitchen solutions for both residential and commercial environments.
